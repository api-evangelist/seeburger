# SEEBURGER

> SEEBURGER Business Integration Suite (BIS) is a cloud-native platform that connects everything from B2B/EDI and MFT to APIs, SaaS-to-SaaS and ERP systems – with built-in AI to accelerate design, automate smarter, and simplify integration management.

## About This Platform
The SEEBURGER Business Integration Suite (BIS) brings together everything you need to connect systems, automate processes, and scale intelligently – in one cloud-native platform.
Designed for the realities of enterprise IT, BIS simplifies integration across SaaS, on-premises and hybrid environments. It empowers both business and technical teams to work together through intuitive tools and intelligent automation – without the overhead.
